Hi, I'm Rozi. I'm an engineering student specializing in networks, cybersecurity and AI security.

At twelve, I read the story of how Turing and his team broke Enigma. That story is why I fell for tech and security, and I haven't really stopped since.

## What I work on

- **AI security**: testing LLM applications against the OWASP Top 10 for LLMs, and studying privacy attacks (membership and attribute inference) on medical data.
- **Networks and infrastructure**: I work alongside my studies as a network and infrastructure consultant, and I'm preparing the CCNA.
- **Security and compliance**: I like connecting the technical side with frameworks such as ISO 27001 and the AI Act.
- **CTFs and labs**: I learn by doing, and I'm preparing Security+.

## Why this blog

I'll publish CTF write-ups, lab notes and what I learn along the way, including what went wrong. Everything here is my own work and opinion, and never includes confidential information about employers or clients.
